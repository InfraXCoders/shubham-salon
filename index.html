Our Story

A family salon built on skill & trust.

NS Unisex Hair Studio 1929 is Fazilka's go-to destination for hair that looks and feels exceptional. Located inside The Royal Mart on Firni Road, our studio welcomes men, women and kids for everything from a sharp everyday cut to complete hair transformations.

Our stylists combine classic technique with modern trends — and use professional-grade products — so you walk out confident, every single time. It's why every one of our 57 Google reviews is five stars.
